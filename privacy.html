Life Budget Pro Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Life Budget Pro (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the app.

Summary

Life Budget Pro stores your data locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit your financial data to our servers.

Information We Collect

  • User-entered data: budgets, transactions, goals, and related notes you enter in the app. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Support communications: if you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you provide.

How We Use Information

  • To provide core app features (budgets, goals, and insights).
  • To respond to support requests.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your data stays on your device unless you choose to share it (for example, by emailing support).

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS security features (such as device encryption and app sandboxing). We recommend using a device passcode or biometric lock for additional protection.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data.

Children’s Privacy

Life Budget Pro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
